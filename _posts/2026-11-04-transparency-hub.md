In a [post last year](https://smcateer.github.io/portfolio/financial-audit-dashboards/) about our financial audit dashboards, I mentioned that I wanted to bring them together on a more unified platform. I finished with "Watch this space!" Well, here it is.

VAGO has now launched its [Transparency Hub](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/transparency-hub), bringing together interactive dashboards that allow Parliament and the public to explore data on the performance of the Victorian public sector.

The hub currently includes seven dashboards covering government service delivery, state finances, major projects, responses to audit recommendations, the financial performance of councils and educational institutions, and spending on consultants.

These dashboards make information that might otherwise be buried in lengthy reports and tables more accessible. Users can explore trends, compare results and dig into the underlying data themselves.

## Building it a step at a time

The Transparency Hub didn't just emerge immediately from one large project. Over several years, my team has worked with auditors across VAGO to develop dashboards supporting individual audits and reports. Each project delivered something useful in its own right, while also helping us build the technical capability and experience to tackle more ambitious projects.

I recently presented on this journey at [IMPACT 2026](https://www.acag.org.au/impact-conference), a conference for performance auditors hosted by VAGO in Melbourne. My message to other audit offices was that you don't need to make one great leap forward. Starting with relatively modest projects, delivering useful results and building capability along the way can eventually produce something much more substantial.

We'll continue improving the existing dashboards and already have plans to add a couple of new ones in the coming year.
